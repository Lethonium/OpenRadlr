OpenRadlr is indoor cycling for your smart trainer: ride an island world together with friends, strangers and bots. No subscription, no account, no ads, and the code is open source.

CONNECTS OVER BLUETOOTH
• Smart trainers that speak Bluetooth FTMS or Cycling Power, like a Wahoo KICKR Core or a Van Rysel D500. A power meter on a classic trainer works too.
• Heart rate straps.
• Controllers for shifting and steering: Zwift Click, Play and Ride, and OpenBikeControl.
• Known devices reconnect by themselves when you open the app.

ONE ISLAND, EVERYONE ON IT
Pick a route and ride the same roads as everyone else on the server: the valley, the cliffs, a col and three cafés. The trainer's resistance follows the gradient.

NEVER AN EMPTY ROAD
Bots ride along when it's quiet, each with its own fitness and style. They're always marked as bots and make room as people join.

DRAFTING, RACES AND EVENTS
Sit on a wheel to save watts. Group rides and races run on a schedule: join one during its countdown, then go for the climbs and the sprints.

SPIN STUDIO
A group class starts every 30 minutes. Coach Lemon calls the blocks, targets follow your FTP, and the room board ranks time on target.

WORKOUTS IN ERG MODE
Ride the built-in sessions or import your own .zwo files: the trainer holds the watts. An FTP test sets your zones.

BODYWEIGHT WORKOUTS
Short circuits of 4 to 15 minutes at the café's outdoor gym, each move shown step by step. No bike needed.

VIRTUAL SHIFTING
Gears for single-speed setups like the Zwift Cog. Shift on screen or with a Zwift Click, Play or Ride.

STRAVA AND RIDE FILES
Finish a ride and upload it to Strava as a virtual ride, with its climbing and a map. Or save it as .fit, .gpx or .tcx.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your profile, ride history and settings stay on your phone. Your heart rate only leaves it inside a ride you upload to Strava. What a ride needs is kept in the server's memory and forgotten when you leave.

The app opens app.openradlr.com and needs an internet connection. Want your own server for your club or your home? OpenRadlr runs in a single Docker container: github.com/Lethonium/OpenRadlr
