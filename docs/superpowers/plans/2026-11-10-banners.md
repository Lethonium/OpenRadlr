# Banners Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Names on the arches, banners over the road counting down climbs and at halfway, and a banner on screen for personal milestones.

**Architecture:** Client only. A pixel font made of geometry writes on arch beams and new banners, built with the world; a pure milestone watch over the ride and the ride history drives a HUD banner. No server or protocol change.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-banners-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Branch `feat/banners`, based on `origin/main`.
- World text is language-neutral; HUD text through `t()` / `tn()` with keys in `en.js` and `de.js`.
- `make check` before every commit.

## Tasks

- [x] **1. Pixel font** — `client/test/banners.test.js` first: every glyph 5 × 7; every banner and arch text writable; text width; centred squares facing the reader. Then `view3d/pixelFont.js`.
- [x] **2. Banners** — `banners.test.js`: halfway on loops (not short loops or non-loops); a long climb's 1 km and 500 m banners, a short climb's that fit; none at an arch; banners high enough to ride under; arches carry their names. Then `view3d/banners.js` and `buildArches`.
- [x] **3. Milestones** — `client/test/milestones.test.js`: totals; steps; 100 km once; round miles; the biggest of several; Everests; nothing before the totals; first summit, not again, not when known, not on a jump. Then `ride/milestones.js`.
- [x] **4. Ride wiring** — `main.js`, the banner element and styles, i18n keys.
- [x] **5. Check in the real app** — ride up Col de la Forge past the KOM arch, both countdown banners and the summit; screenshots in the PR.
