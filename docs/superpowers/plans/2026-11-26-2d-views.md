# 2D views (map and side view from the 3D world) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the 2D ride map with two 2D views, a top-down Map and a layered Side view, both drawn from the 3D world's scene with sprites rendered on the device from the real 3D assets.

**Architecture:** A new `js/view2d/` folder. Pure modules index the world scene, project it onto the route, sample the ground and pick colours; a sprite baker (the only Three.js user in the folder) renders workbench assets into canvases, fed by a budgeted sprite store; `mapView.js` and `sideView.js` draw with Canvas 2D; `View2D` is the controller `main.js` talks to, next to `View3D`.

**Tech Stack:** plain ES modules, Canvas 2D, vendored Three.js (baker only), node:test.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-2d-views-design.md`. Prototype to port from (throwaway, local branch `spike/2d-views`): `client/public/js/spike2d/` — `data.js` (index, projector), `palette.js`, `topdown.js`, `side.js`, `baker.js`, `sprites.js`, `cam.js` (use `low` only). Read it with `git show spike/2d-views:client/public/js/spike2d/<file>`.

- Scene fixture for tests: `sceneOf(buildSceneNow(tinyLoopFile(), [], tinyNetworkFile()))` with `new RouteGeometry(tinyLoopFile())` (see `test/plan.test.js:227`); fake 2D canvas: move `fakeCanvas` from `test/rideMapView.test.js` to `test/support/fakeCanvas.js` (Task 2) and extend it to record `drawImage` calls.
- Starting values from the spec (named constants, one place each): side camera distance `CAM_M = 110`, horizon at `0.3` of the height above the road, road at `0.64` of the height, grade exaggeration `VEX = 1.6` flattening to `0.32` far away, `SPAN_M = 55` of road across, my rider at `0.3` from the left, building turn `HOUSE_TILT = -0.45` rad, scenery reach `70` m behind / `22` m in front, side sprite pitch `0.16` rad; map `MAP_SPAN_M = 95` tall, rider at `0.58` of the height, riders drawn `2.4×`, tiles `64` m at `6` px/m; sprite memory cap `40 MB`.

## Review Focus

1. **A season, time or weather change mid-ride:** the views follow on the next frame; a switch to dusk/night asks for the night bakes and shows the day sprites until they are ready (Task 3 test).
2. **A new rider look appears mid-ride (a bot pack of 100, a joiner):** each look is baked once, in the budget; until then the rider is drawn with the default look's sprite, never skipped (Task 3 test).
3. **The baker fails (context lost, no WebGL2 after all, an asset throws):** one failing asset is skipped and logged; a baker that cannot start makes `View2D.available` false and `main.js` falls back to today's `drawRideMap` (Tasks 1 and 8 tests).
4. **A route that is not a loop, or its ends:** the side view clamps distances at both ends; nothing is drawn past the end, no NaN (Task 6 test).
5. **Canvas resized or a new device pixel ratio:** cached bands and tiles at the old scale are dropped and remade (Tasks 5 and 6 tests).
