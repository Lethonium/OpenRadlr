# Bots With Character: Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Bots are characters who ride with and against the humans: a cast with riding types and team kits, moves within a W′ energy budget, and groups that spawn around every rider (#64).

**Architecture:** Mostly the sim crate.
- `names.rs` holds the cast.
- `bot.rs` holds the types, teams, energy and moves.
- `world.rs` works out what each bot sees each step and decides where groups spawn.

The server adds three optional fields to bots' rider entries. The client tints bots' jerseys, names their type in the rider list and calls out moves in the ticker. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-bots-design.md`. Decision: ADR 0022.

## Steps

1. **Cast (`sim/names.rs`):** made-up names, a first name and a cycling-pun surname ("Gus Tailwind"). Keep `generate_unique`, add `name_hash` (FNV-1a). Easter egg: one new bot in 200 is "Goldie Lemon", always in Team Zitrone. Tests: names are a first name and a pun surname, the cast holds at least 1,000 names, uniqueness and the numbered fallback, a stable hash.
2. **Types and teams (`sim/bot.rs`):**
   - `RidingStyle::{Rouleur, Climber, Sprinter, Attacker, Tourer}` and `Team`, both from the name hash.
   - `BotProfile::character` and `BotProfile::matched` take the name.
   - Tests: a name is stable; the shares are roughly as planned; weights suit the type.
3. **Energy (`Energy`):** spend above CP, recover below with Skiba's τ. Tests: spend, clamp at empty, faster recovery when riding easier, the exact value for one case.
4. **Moves (`Move`, `BotBrain::decide`, `choose_lane`):** one move at a time, started from `BotSituation` and ended on time or energy; lane wishes for answer and swing off; escaping a blocked lane while attacking. Tests:
   - a climber attacks near the top only with a human close, then recovers;
   - each line once per pass;
   - a sprinter goes for the line only with an audience;
   - a tired bot lets the line go;
   - an attacker answers a human, not a bot;
   - a pull, then a swing off, then back once passed;
   - no moves in a vehicle or a group ride;
   - only pull, attack, answer and sprint go on the wire.
5. **What bots see (`World::bot_surroundings`):** the next line, a rider coming past, a wheel behind, the gap ahead, the nearest human, waiting for or leaving the group. Tests on a route with a climb and a sprint, and on a spawned group.
6. **Spawning (`route_target`, `keep_company`, `make_room`, `spawn_group`):** groups in rows, company ahead or behind out of sight, far bots make room. Tests:
   - the target follows the humans, with its cap;
   - no bots with a target of 0;
   - company out of sight;
   - company levels ahead and behind;
   - a full route brings its bots to a lonely human;
   - racers bring no company;
   - groups in rows at about one level.

   Adjust the old tests: base population without company, and "groups start apart".
7. **Tuning:** a simulated hour per route with one rider at 75% of a 3.0 W/kg FTP. Bring spawns closer (350–800 m ahead), and stop counting bots more than 400 m behind as company. The numbers are in the spec.
8. **Protocol (`server/protocol.rs`):** `team`, `style` and `move` on bots, none on humans. Test it, and update `docs/protocol.md`.
9. **Client:**
   - `view/botKits.js`: kits avoiding orange and sky blue; the 3D view tints bots' jerseys.
   - `panels.botStyle.*`: the type in the rider list badge. The name and its badges sit apart in the row, so only the name is cut short.
   - `ride/botCalls.js`: calls the start of a move near me, rate limited; wired into the `state` handler.
   - i18n in English and German.
   - Tests for kits, the badge, the calls and the 3D tint.
10. **Docs:** the spec, this plan, ADR 0022, `architecture.md`, CHANGELOG. Screenshots for the PR from a local server.
